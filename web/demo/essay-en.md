# SYNTHETIC: Water balance in semi-arid basins

Course: HYD-101. This assignment must be submitted through the virtual campus before the published deadline.

Semi-arid basins lose most of their water to the atmosphere. As one study puts it, “evapotranspiration accounts for most of the water leaving the basin” (Smith, 2020, p. 47). Measurements at three synthetic stations agree with that view.

Rainfall in these basins is irregular. Local farmers often say that “rainfall arrives in a few intense events each year” and that the rest of the year is dry.

Groundwater recharge happens mostly in wet years, when soils are saturated for several weeks (García y López, 2019). This makes long records essential.

In an earlier essay I described how small check dams slow runoff and let water soak into the riverbed during the brief floods of late summer.

The synthetic stations were installed in 2025 and record rainfall, soil moisture and river level every fifteen minutes. Their data are summarised in the appendix of this essay.
