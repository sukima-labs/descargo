# Procedures: how a student defends themselves in Mexico

*[Español](../procedimientos-mx.md) · English*

This guide explains, with sources, **what stages the formal procedure has** when a Mexican
university accuses you of plagiarism or academic fraud on the strength of a detector, and
**where Descargo fits** in each. Spain and Colombia are in
[`procedures.md`](procedures.md), and Argentina in
[`procedures-ar.md`](procedures-ar.md), Chile in [`procedures-cl.md`](procedures-cl.md),
and Peru in [`procedures-pe.md`](procedures-pe.md).

**This is not legal advice.** It is public information put in order. Every fact carries
its source and the date a person checked it by reading the official text. What could not
be checked is marked **to verify**. Every university also has its own rules, which take
precedence over the general ones: read yours in full before replying to anything.

A detector's percentage is **an indication**, not a sanction. To sanction you, the
university has to open a procedure and let you defend yourself within it.

## What applies (checked 2026-11-08)

**There is no national law on university discipline.** Each autonomous university sets its
own:

- The **Constitution**, art. 3, section VII: the universities to which the law grants
  autonomy «tendrán la facultad y la responsabilidad de gobernarse a sí mismas» (shall have
  the power and the responsibility to govern themselves)
  ([CPEUM](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/CPEUM.pdf), last amended in the DOF
  2026-10-07).
- The **Ley General de Educación Superior** (DOF 2021-04-20), art. 2: the autonomous ones
  «se regirán por sus respectivas leyes orgánicas, la normatividad que deriva de éstas y,
  en lo que resulte compatible, por las disposiciones de la presente Ley» (shall be governed
  by their own organic laws, the rules derived from them and, where compatible, by this
  Law). Among the criteria of higher education policy is the «fomento de la integridad
  académica» (promotion of academic integrity) (art. 10, section VI)
  ([LGES](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LGES_200421.pdf)).
- The Constitution, art. 14: nobody may be deprived of their rights except through a trial
  «en el que se cumplan las formalidades esenciales del procedimiento y conforme a las
  Leyes expedidas con anterioridad al hecho» (in which the essential formalities of the
  procedure are met, in accordance with laws issued before the act). How this applies, in
  concrete terms, to a university sanction is **to verify** with a professional (see
  [amparo, below](#if-the-university-does-not-respect-your-rights)).

So **the stages, the deadlines and the offences come from your university's own rules**.
What follows is the example of the UNAM, the largest in the country. Use it to know what to
look for in yours, not as if it were yours.

## Example: the UNAM

Sources: the **Estatuto General**, arts. 93 to 101
([text](https://www.abogadogeneral.unam.mx:6060/legislacion/view/1)), and the **Reglamento
del Tribunal Universitario**, amended on 2025-11-27
([text](https://www.abogadogeneral.unam.mx:6060/legislacion/view/138)), both on the website
of the Oficina de la Abogacía General.

### What counts as an offence

| Conduct | Sanction | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Having «prestado o recibido ayuda fraudulenta en las pruebas de aprovechamiento» (given or received fraudulent help in achievement tests) | Suspension for up to one year, «sin perjuicio de la nulidad del examen sustentado» (without prejudice to the annulment of the exam taken) | Estatuto, 97.II |
| Fraudulent help «para la titulación y/o examen de grado» (for the degree and/or the degree exam) | Review process and, where appropriate, annulment of the exam, besides the sanctions in the Estatuto | Estatuto, 97.III |
| Forging certificates, exam transcripts and similar documents | Expulsion | Estatuto, 97.IV |

If the offence has no sanction specified, the options are a reprimand, suspension or
removal from posts, suspension of university rights for up to one year, or definitive
expulsion (Estatuto, 98.II).

**The Estatuto General does not use the word «plagio»** (checked on 2026-11-10 in its
current text). Which article would apply to you depends on what you are charged with, and
the Estatuto does not say. What it does contain:

| What | Article |
|---|---|
| Everyone in the University must observe the principles of its **Código de Ética** (code of ethics) | 7 |
| Before graduating you sign the **protest of academic integrity and honesty**: you accept that, if a breach is detected, «se dejen sin efectos los actos realizados» (the acts done are left without effect) | 87.V |
| The University may declare the **absolute nullity** of the certificate, diploma, title or degree if the requirements of the rules were not met; the person affected may choose another graduation route | 5 (para. 2) and 87.VI |
| «Actos contrarios a la moral, a la ética y al respeto» (acts contrary to morals, ethics and respect) among university members is an especially serious cause of liability (article amended on 2020-02-12 and 2025-03-31) | 95.VII |
| A **teacher's** lack of academic integrity and honesty in supervising theses and papers | 96.III |

None of these articles names copying a written work or sets its sanction. For students,
the conduct with a sanction of its own is that in the table above: fraudulent help **in
tests** (97.II) and **for graduating** (97.III). When the offence has no sanction
specified, 98.II applies.

**Beware of old citations.** The UNAM's Faculty of Law explains plagiarism through arts.
87.I, 95.I and VI and 97.II
([the Faculty's guide](https://www.derecho.unam.mx/integridad-academica/pdf/enfrentando_el_plagio.pdf)),
and says students who commit it «podrán ser reprobados» (may fail the course) and be
suspended for up to a year. That is one faculty's reading, not a rule, and its numbering
does not match the current text: there, 95.VI is carrying weapons and the «moral y ética»
paragraph is VII.

If they charge you with an offence, ask them to tell you **which article and which
specific fact** you are charged with, and which sanction they intend to apply: it is a
point for your reply. The rules of your faculty or programme may also regulate plagiarism
in a written work.

### The stages of the procedure (Reglamento, art. 2)

Deadlines are counted in **working days** of the school calendar (Reglamento, art. 3).

1. **Initial.** It begins in your faculty or school, with a report or complaint to its head
   or on the faculty's own initiative (art. 2.I).
2. **Investigation.** Your faculty carries it out, within up to **twenty working days**,
   extendable by a reasoned decision. It ends with a decision that sends the file to the
   Tribunal Universitario, declares the case inadmissible, imposes a reprimand or decides
   that a conciliation agreement is appropriate (art. 14 bis). Before notifying you of a
   provisional sanction, the faculty has to ask for the observations of the Oficina de la
   Abogacía General, which also checks «el respeto de los derechos de la persona remitida»
   (respect for the rights of the person referred) (art. 14).
   - The head of your faculty or school can **reprimand you** at once, or **suspend or
     expel you provisionally**. In that case the matter must go to the Tribunal within six
     days at most (Estatuto, art. 93).
3. **Referral to the Tribunal.** The faculty sends the file within a maximum of **six
   working days** from notifying you (art. 14). The file must carry «el total de los
   elementos de prueba» (all the evidence) (art. 17). Within ten working days, the Tribunal
   admits it, asks for it to be completed or **sends it back** if it sees acts that violate
   due process (art. 17.III).
4. **Notification.** They send you the admission, with a copy of the referral document and
   «el total de las pruebas que integran la investigación» (all the evidence that makes up
   the investigation), and inform you of your rights (art. 19). It reaches you first by
   email (art. 18).
5. **Your reply.** You have **ten working days** to reply in writing, offer evidence and
   present closing arguments (art. 23.I). Evidence is offered by stating which fact each
   item proves (art. 20).
6. **Preliminary hearing.** It is oral and, with exceptions, public. The evidence is taken
   and the arguments are put forward in it (art. 25).
7. **Resolution.** The Sala (Chamber) upholds, modifies or revokes what your faculty
   decided, and the resolution must be «debidamente fundada y motivada» (duly founded and
   reasoned) (art. 29). The Tribunal assesses the evidence freely (Estatuto, art. 101).
8. **Review.** The Tribunal's resolution can be challenged by a review appeal (*recurso de
   revisión*) before the **Comisión de Honor** of the Consejo Universitario, in writing and
   within a maximum of **five working days** of notification (art. 31). If you were only
   reprimanded, you ask for the review before the Tribunal itself (art. 32; Estatuto,
   art. 93).

### Your rights before the Tribunal (Reglamento, art. 23)

- To reply in writing and offer evidence and closing arguments within ten working days.
- To have an adviser or legal representative. If you do not have one, the **Defensoría de
  los Derechos Universitarios, Igualdad y Atención de la Violencia de Género** advises you
  on which public institution can provide one.
- To **know the contents of the file**, by consulting it at the Tribunal.
- To challenge (seek the recusal of) any member of the Sala.
- To attend and take part in the preliminary hearing.

## If the university does not respect your rights

The **juicio de amparo** (amparo trial) is the federal route to challenge acts that violate
fundamental rights
([Ley de Amparo](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LAmp.pdf), last amended in
the DOF 2025-10-16). What the law says:

- An authority responsible for the act is whoever issues an act that «crea, modifica o
  extingue situaciones jurídicas en forma unilateral y obligatoria» (creates, modifies or
  extinguishes legal situations unilaterally and compulsorily), «con independencia de su
  naturaleza formal» (regardless of its formal nature), and a private party can also be one
  when it performs acts equivalent to those of an authority (art. 5, section II).
- The general deadline to file the claim is **fifteen days** (art. 17), counted from the
  day after the one on which the notification takes effect (art. 18).
- Beforehand, as a general rule, you must exhaust the remedies that can modify or revoke
  the act (art. 61, section XX), such as the review before the Comisión de Honor at the
  UNAM.

**To verify:** whether a disciplinary sanction from your university, public or private, can
be challenged in amparo depends on how the courts treat it, and this guide has not checked
that in the case law. See a professional before the fifteen days run out.

## Where Descargo fits

| Stage | What Descargo can do |
|---|---|
| Before submitting | `inspect` lists what the file carries besides the visible text |
| Investigation | `access-request` drafts the request for access to your data (ARCO rights), including the detector's report: to the Unidad de Transparencia if the university is public (LGPDPPSO) or to the university itself if it is private (LFPDPPP). See [`jurisdictions/mx/`](../../jurisdictions/mx/pack.yaml) |
| Your reply (10 working days at the UNAM) and preliminary hearing | `import-report` and `audit` help read the report and recalculate the percentage without quotations, bibliography or templates |
